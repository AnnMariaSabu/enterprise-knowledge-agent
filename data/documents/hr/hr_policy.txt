ACME CORP - HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY
Version 2.1 - Applies to all employees

1. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a
one-hour lunch break. Employees may work flexible hours between 7:00 AM
and 7:00 PM with their manager's approval.

2. SICK LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
Part-time employees receive sick leave in proportion to their hours.
For example, an employee working 3 days a week receives 6 days per year.
A doctor's note is required if you are sick for more than 3 days in a row.
Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Annual leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance through the
HR portal. Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the
next year. Any remaining days are lost on 31 December.

4. PARENTAL LEAVE
Birth mothers receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
Fathers and partners receive 2 weeks of paid paternity leave.
Parental leave must be requested at least 8 weeks before the start date.

5. PROBATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
New employees have a probation period of 6 months.
During probation, the notice period is 1 week.
After probation, the notice period is 1 month for both sides.

6. CONTACT
For questions about this policy, email hr@acmecorp.example.
