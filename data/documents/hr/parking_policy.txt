ACME CORP - PARKING POLICY
Version 1.0 - Applies to all employees

1. WHERE TO PARK
Employees can park for free in the underground garage, levels B1 and B2.
Level B1 is reserved for visitors and employees with disabilities.
All other employees should park on level B2.

2. ELECTRIC CARS
Electric car charging is available on level B2.
Charging costs 2 US dollars per hour.
Please move your car when charging is complete so others can use the charger.

3. PARKING PASSES
Parking passes are issued by HR on your first day.
A lost parking pass can be replaced for 10 US dollars.

4. MOTORBIKES AND BICYCLES
Motorbikes and bicycles can be parked at the side entrance.
Bicycle parking is free and has space for 40 bicycles.
