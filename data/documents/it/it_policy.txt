ACME CORP - IT POLICY
Version 1.4 - Applies to all employees

1. PASSWORDS
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a number and
a symbol. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. You cannot reuse any
of your last 5 passwords. Never share your password with anyone,
including the IT team.

2. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, the HR portal
and the VPN. Use the Acme Authenticator app on your phone.

3. VPN
You must connect to the company VPN when working outside the office,
including from home, hotels, airports and cafes. The VPN is not required
when you are connected to the office network.

4. LAPTOPS AND DEVICES
Every employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are replaced every
3 years. You may not install software yourself; request new software
through the IT helpdesk. A lost or stolen device must be reported to IT
within 24 hours.

5. IT HELPDESK
The IT helpdesk is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Email: helpdesk@acmecorp.example
Phone extension: 4357
For urgent problems outside these hours, call the on-call number 555-0100.
