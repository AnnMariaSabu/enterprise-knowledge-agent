ACME CORP - TRAVEL POLICY
Version 3.0 - Applies to all employees travelling for work

1. APPROVAL
All business trips must be approved by your manager before booking.
Trips longer than 5 days also need approval from your department director.

2. FLIGHTS
Book flights at least 14 days before the trip to get lower prices.
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours.
Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer, with
director approval.

3. HOTELS
The hotel limit is 150 US dollars per night for domestic trips.
The hotel limit is 220 US dollars per night for international trips.
Book hotels through the Acme Travel portal whenever possible.

4. MEALS
The meal allowance is 50 US dollars per day for domestic trips.
The meal allowance is 75 US dollars per day for international trips.
Alcohol is not reimbursed.

5. EXPENSES
Submit your expense report within 30 days after the trip ends.
Attach receipts for every expense above 25 US dollars.
Late expense reports may not be reimbursed.

6. TRAVEL INSURANCE
All employees on approved business trips are automatically covered
by company travel insurance. No action is needed.
